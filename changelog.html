What's new

The most recent changes will be listed first. Not every small fix makes the list — just the bits actually worth knowing about.

24 October 2026
  • New Being sentences (X は Y です) in the Sentence builder, both challenges and a fourth Desk sheet: jobs (たなかさんは けんちくしです — "Tanaka-san is an architect"), nationalities (イギリスじん) and describing words (えいがは おもしろいです). The verb column becomes です, じゃありません, でした, じゃありませんでした (+か), and the time column いま, きょねん, らいねん. い-words like おもしろい only take です for now — their own negative and past come later — and the builder says so if you try.
  • The Sentence order card has Being too (Doing / Going / Giving / Being): tap the topic to skip it or ask だれが…?, the time on or off (time words go with jobs: きょねん かいしゃいんでした), and the verb for か.
  • Fixed: the topic particle は is now always read out as "wa" — ははは (my mother + は) was being read "haha ha", or even like laughing. Applies to the Sentence builder and challenges, the Sentence order card, the opening screen's sentence and the particle drill.
23 October 2026
  • New 作文 tab ("composition"), between Vocab and Numbers, for putting sentences together: the Sentence builder, how each part of your sentences is going, the Sentence order card and the Desk sheets. The Grammar tab is now just the grammar notes. Numbers moved one place to the right. On it, Sentence order and the Desk sheets come first, then the Sentence builder and the Sentence challenge as two separate cards, with your part scores under the challenge.
  • Fixed: after finishing a challenge, the Sentence builder's cards couldn't be tapped.
  • The tabs are now all in Japanese — かな, 漢字, ことば (words), さくぶん (composition), すうじ (numbers), ぶんぽう (grammar) — with English page titles (Vocabulary, Composition…), so you read them every day. They'll switch to kanji as the kanji are taught.
  • Your own family turns up much more in the made-up sentences (challenges, Sentence order, the opening screen): about a quarter of sentences are about one of your family, and they're often with another of your family (ははは ちちと…). Other people's family and the named people come up a bit less.
  • New Reading challenge on the さくぶん tab: a Japanese sentence to read, answered by picking the English for each part. The cards lead with English, then romaji; kana only if you ask for it, and the romaji of the sentence is there as a hint. It keeps its own part scores, separate from the Sentence challenge.
  • The Sentence builder now shows the English of what you've built as you go, with a gentle note when a time word doesn't match the verb's tense (きのう with 〜ます) or あまり / ぜんぜん is used with a positive verb.
  • On a laptop or other wide screen the Sentence builder and challenge now fill the screen, with bigger text, and are the same width as each other.
  • Sentence challenges now score each part of the sentence (who, when, how often, with whom, where, what, verb, verb ending) instead of each word: the Grammar page shows how often you get each part right first time, and the builder points at the part with most room to grow. The thin teal bars on word tiles are gone.
  • Sentence builder: tapping a word now says it with its particle (ともだちと, コンビニで), and the verb with its ending. Answer cards use the same short English as the others ("pool", not "at the pool"), and the romaji no longer runs into its underlines when it wraps onto a second line.
  • Sentence builder cards now show the romaji brighter than the English, so you find words by their sound first.
  • On Android phones the Sentence builder and Desk sheets can now turn sideways; the rest of the app stays upright.
  • Sentence builder challenge: the answer card now looks exactly like the others ("on Saturday", not "on Saturdays"; romaji in the same style), and names are written with a capital everywhere (Watanabe-san).
22 October 2026
  • Typing answers now has its own score. A correct typed answer still adds to a word's usual score, but a wrong or nearly-right one never takes anything away from it. Instead it feeds a separate "typed from memory" score, shown as a thin teal bar along the bottom of the word's tile and under each word list's progress bar. It's graded upwards: a perfect answer earns the most, one slip ("Nearly!") most of it, an answer after a hint half, and a wrong answer barely moves it.
  • New Sentence builder on the Grammar tab: the desk sheets you can tap. Build any sentence one column at a time and hear it, or take the Challenge — an English sentence to build in Japanese, checked part by part, with a second go for anything not right. Challenge answers count towards each word's typed-from-memory score. Best with your phone sideways.
  • Sentence builder challenge: the right verb with the wrong ending now says "right verb — check the ending" (wavy underline) and keeps the verb picked, and a word you pick again after a check no longer keeps the old crossed-out look. The answer's own card always has its English now, so it doesn't stand out. いま in a place column now says "living room", not "now" (also on the desk sheets).
  • The opening screen no longer ends with a Japanese saying: it's now just the greeting, date and time, and a sentence to read, each in its own colour (a different pair each time).
20 October 2026
  • Level 3 Lesson 4 (giving and receiving): about 60 new words, including あげます / もらいます, かします / かります and おしえます / ならいます, a new Occasions list (たんじょうび, クリスマス, おみまい…), and gift, visiting and adjective words.
  • New grammar note: Giving and receiving — who gets に, and why nobody あげます to you.
  • Sentence order has a third kind of sentence, Giving & receiving: who → when → how often → to / from whom に → what を → verb, with あげます / もらいます, かします / かります, おしえます / ならいます, and writing, sending and phoning. The time slot includes occasions like たんじょうびに and クリスマスに.
  • Desk sheets have a third page, Giving & receiving, and the sentence on the start screen now sometimes gives, lends, teaches or phones too.
  • Fixed: printing the Desk sheets could add a blank page.
18 October 2026
  • Small tidy-ups so this app matches Kanji cards and Kanji strokes: the app name's colours, the Settings wording, and up-to-date descriptions of the two kanji apps.
17 October 2026
  • Hold a course or tourist word on any word list (including search results) and a gold strip under the bubble writes it out stroke by stroke.
  • Kana stroke order: on every kana page — base, dakuten / handakuten and contracted — touch and hold a character to see it drawn stroke by stroke. Contracted kana like きゃ are drawn as the pair.
16 October 2026
  • Proper back navigation: inner pages have a "← …" link at the top left saying where it goes, and your phone's back gesture now goes one step back too (on a tab's main page it still does nothing, so you can't leave the app by accident). The ⚙ button closes Settings when tapped again. The Vocab and Numbers tabs no longer turn into "Back".
  • Grammar is now its own tab along the top, next to Vocab. To make room, the ⚙ and sound buttons have moved up beside the app's name.
  • Untested 20 only lists the kinds of word you still have left to try.
15 October 2026
  • New Grammar pill on the Vocab screen: every grammar note on one page, with the sentence order cards and Desk sheets first and the rest newest first. The Grammar notes cards have gone from the L2 and L3 lists, and Desk sheets has moved here from Settings.
  • Grammar notes are easier to read, especially in dark mode. The Grammar page's links are plainer, so the headings stand out, and the two older Level 2 cards (で and question word + も) now look like the rest.
  • Level 3 is now teal instead of brown, so it no longer looks like a darker Level 2. On the dice page, Untested 20 shows how many words you haven't tried yet, and picks course words first, then tourist words, then the rest of Untaught.
  • New Tourist pill with its own switch in Settings: the trip words and phrases, in their eight sections, separate from the rest of Untaught. Tourist and Untaught now sit on a second row under the main pills, so they no longer scroll off the edge. If you had Untaught on, Tourist is switched on for you. A line at the top of the Tourist, Untaught and Kanji pages says which Settings switch turns each one off.
  • Fixed: switching Untaught vocabulary off in Settings now stays off. For some people it was turning itself back on every time the app reloaded.
  • Settings now links to Kanji cards as well as Kanji strokes.
  • The two Level 2 Sentence order cards have been retired — the Level 3 card does everything they did, and its "Going somewhere" mode now uses きます (come) too: to my house, to school, to a party.
  • Level 3 Sentence order card: the English is now coloured to match the slots, so you can see how the order changes between Japanese and English. Invitations with the object left out now say "Let's do it" rather than "Let's do".
  • Sentence order cards: a question word (だれと, どこで…) now always adds か, and the English asks the question naturally ("Where is she going by bus, and with whom?").
13 October 2026
  • Level 3 Lesson 3: about 50 new words in three new categories (Gifts & souvenirs, Adjectives, Phrases & replies) and the existing ones (including にほんご and えいご), plus a new Inviting grammar card.
  • Verbs now show their inviting forms, ませんか and ましょう, in the hold-and-slide window and in the Verb forms drill. On the Sentence order card, keep tapping the verb to get an invitation or "let's".
  • The daily sentence now includes Lesson 3 conversations: inviting and replying, arranging when and where to meet, and choosing a birthday present together.
  • Desk sheets (in Settings): two printable A4 pages to keep in front of you in class — the sentence order across the top and taught words under each part, so you can pick a word and concentrate on the grammar. Shuffle for a different set of words.
  • Not installed yet? Settings now shows how to install the app on your phone (and an Install button where the phone offers one). It disappears once it's installed.
  • New on the dice page: Small っ — hear きて or きって (and other pairs) and pick which one it was.

New are words like "maybe", "more" and "not yet", position words (on, under, behind, opposite), body parts and pharmacy words, and travel kit like charger and power bank.

The tense follows the time word. The daily sentence and the Particles drill now include "with someone" (と) in more sentences too.

  • Level 3's Sentence order card now shares its words and rules with the daily sentence, and "Try another example" changes the frequency word too.
