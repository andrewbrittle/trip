INSTRUCTIONS

How this works

Getting around
Tap a date in the grid up top to jump to that day, or use the Previous/Next buttons at the bottom of each day. When you're on the trip, the highlighted date is today, based on your device's clock.
Cities and places
The coloured bar across the top is split into Bucharest and Sofia — tap a city's name there to open its Wikipedia page. Wherever you see the pin icon next to a place, tap it to open that spot in Google Maps.
Marking things off
Tap the circle on any card to cycle it through not done → done (✓) → skipped (✕) and back. Done/skipped cards collapse to keep the day short — tap again to bring the detail back.
More detail
Where a card has a small ⓘ next to its title, tap it for extra background or history on that place.
Adding your own
Each day has an "Add something" box at the bottom for anything new that comes up on the day — it gets its own card, with a × to remove it if it doesn't work out. There's a Notes box underneath for anything else worth remembering.
Sending changes back
Settings (⚙) → Export updates gathers everything you've added, ticked off, or noted into one block of text. Copy it (or download it as a file) and paste it into a chat with Claude to have it researched properly and folded into the plan for good.
Currency
The coin icon top-right converts between pounds and either RON or Sofia's euros, at whatever rate you set — pick the currency and direction, then type an amount to see it converted.
Settings
The ⚙ icon top-right opens Settings, where appearance (auto/light/dark) and whether the calming proverb cards show up can be changed, along with this page and the export.
Words
The Words pills next to BUCHAREST and SOFIA in the calendar bar each open a small phrasebook for that language, with how to actually read each phrase out loud.
